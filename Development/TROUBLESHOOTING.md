**Project:** ITH Attendance Management System

**Document Version:** 2.0

**Status:** Approved

# Purpose

This document provides general guidance for investigating development and operational issues.

---

# Troubleshooting Principles

When investigating issues:

- Reproduce the problem.
- Identify the root cause.
- Verify the fix.
- Prevent regression.

---

# Common Investigation Areas

Typical areas include:

- Authentication
- Attendance
- Presence Monitoring
- Leave Management
- Reporting
- Configuration

---

# Diagnostics

Useful diagnostic information may include:

- Application logs
- Audit records
- Error codes
- Request history

---

# Resolution

Resolved issues should be documented where they provide long-term value to future contributors.
