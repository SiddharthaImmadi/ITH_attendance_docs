# Volunteering Policy

**Project:** ITH Attendance Management System

**Document Version:** 2.0

**Status:** Approved

---

# Purpose

This document defines the business rules governing volunteer participation before, during, and after an event.

It specifies volunteer eligibility, participation expectations, temporary restrictions, and administrative authority regarding future volunteering opportunities.

Implementation details such as APIs, database structures, or application logic are intentionally excluded.

---

# Policy Objective

The objective of this policy is to ensure that volunteering opportunities remain fair, accountable, and transparent while recognizing legitimate operational and personal circumstances.

Administrative decisions shall always prioritize the successful execution of the event while treating volunteers consistently.

---

# Volunteer Eligibility

A volunteer may participate in an event only when all of the following conditions are satisfied:

- The volunteer has a valid account.
- The volunteer is authenticated.
- The volunteer is not under an active volunteering restriction.
- The volunteer is assigned to the event.
- The event session is open.

If any condition is not satisfied, participation shall not begin.

---

# Volunteer Responsibilities

Every volunteer is expected to:

- Arrive on time.
- Complete attendance through check-in.
- Remain inside the approved venue unless authorized to leave.
- Respond to attendance notifications.
- Submit truthful leave requests.
- Cooperate with administrators during attendance review.
- Complete attendance through check-out whenever possible.

---

# Expected Conduct

Volunteers shall:

- Follow organizer instructions.
- Participate in assigned responsibilities.
- Use the attendance system honestly.
- Avoid intentionally bypassing attendance monitoring.

Repeated misuse of the attendance system may result in administrative action.

---

# Temporary Volunteering Restrictions

A volunteering restriction is an administrative action that temporarily prevents a volunteer from participating in future volunteering opportunities.

Restrictions shall not be applied automatically.

Every restriction requires administrative review.

---

# Reasons for Restriction

A restriction may be applied when:

- A leave request is rejected.
- Attendance policies are repeatedly violated.
- False or misleading information is submitted.
- Administrative investigation determines misuse of the attendance system.

The administrator may consider previous attendance history before making a decision.

---

# Restriction Effects

While a restriction is active:

- The volunteer shall not register for new volunteering opportunities.
- The volunteer shall not participate in restricted events.
- Existing attendance records remain unchanged.
- Historical reports remain available for administrative review.

Restrictions do not delete or modify historical attendance data.

---

# Removal of Restrictions

Only an administrator may remove a volunteering restriction.

Removal shall be based on organizational policy and administrative judgment.

Every removal shall be recorded for audit purposes.

---

# Administrative Authority

Administrators are responsible for:

- Reviewing attendance history.
- Reviewing leave requests.
- Applying restrictions where appropriate.
- Removing restrictions when justified.
- Maintaining fairness and consistency across volunteers.

Administrative decisions should be supported by recorded attendance history whenever possible.

---

# Fairness Principles

Administrative actions shall:

- Be based on documented evidence.
- Be applied consistently.
- Be reviewable.
- Be recorded for future reference.

Legitimate volunteer circumstances shall be considered during administrative review.

---

# Compliance

Participation in any volunteering event implies acceptance of this policy.

Failure to comply may result in temporary participation restrictions as determined by an administrator.
