
# Acceptance Criteria

**Project:** ITH Attendance Management System

**Document Version:** 2.0

**Status:** Approved

---

# Purpose

This document defines the conditions that must be satisfied before a feature is considered complete.

---

# General Criteria

A feature is accepted when:

- Requirements are implemented.
- Business rules are enforced.
- Validation rules are satisfied.
- Required tests pass.
- Documentation remains consistent.

---

# Functional Acceptance

The implemented behavior shall match the approved documentation.

---

# Non-Functional Acceptance

The implementation shall satisfy applicable security, reliability, and performance expectations.

---

# Traceability

Acceptance shall be traceable to:

- Product requirements
- Business rules
- System design
- API specifications
