
# Logging and Auditing

**Project:** ITH Attendance Management System

**Document Version:** 2.0

**Status:** Approved

---

# Purpose

This document defines logging and audit principles for backend operations.

---

# Logging

The backend should record information useful for:

- Diagnostics
- Operational monitoring
- Error investigation

---

# Audit Logging

Audit records shall capture significant business events.

Examples include:

- Login
- Check-in
- Check-out
- Leave submission
- Leave approval
- Leave rejection
- Restriction changes

---

# Audit Principles

Audit records should:

- Preserve chronological accuracy.
- Record responsible users where applicable.
- Support historical reporting.
