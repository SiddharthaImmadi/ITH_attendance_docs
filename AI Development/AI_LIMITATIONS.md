
# AI Limitations

**Project:** ITH Attendance Management System

**Document Version:** 2.0

**Status:** Approved

---

# Purpose

This document defines the operational limitations of AI assistants within the project.

---

# Decision Making

AI assists with technical implementation but does not replace project decision-making.

Project contributors remain responsible for:

- Business decisions
- Requirement approval
- Architectural approval
- Release approval

---

# Scope Limitations

AI shall not:

- Assume undocumented requirements.
- Invent missing business rules.
- Modify approved documentation without instruction.
- Expand feature scope without approval.

---

# Transparency

When documentation is incomplete or ambiguous, AI should request clarification rather than making assumptions.
